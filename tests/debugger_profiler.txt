# profiler (prof) on tests/profiler/profiler.nes (run by ctest: debugger_profiler; the program: make_rom.py)
# A (C100) is called 3 times and calls B (C110) twice; R (C120) recurses 3 deep; W (C130) waits for the NMI handler
prof on
bpx c029
g
!assert reg pc c029
!assert prof routines 5
!assert prof calls c100 3
!assert prof calls c110 6
# B: NOP NOP RTS = 10 cycles. A: JSR JSR RTS = 18 of its own, 38 with the two B calls
!assert prof excl c110 60
!assert prof incl c110 60
!assert prof min c110 10
!assert prof max c110 10
!assert prof excl c100 54
!assert prof incl c100 114
!assert prof min c100 38
!assert prof max c100 38
# R recurses: 16 + 16 + 11 cycles; Incl counts the outermost activation only
!assert prof calls c120 3
!assert prof excl c120 43
!assert prof incl c120 43
!assert prof min c120 11
!assert prof max c120 43
# the NMI came while W waited: the handler is LDA STA RTI = 11 cycles, and not part of W
!assert prof calls c130 1
!assert prof calls c140 1
!assert prof excl c140 11
!assert prof incl c140 11
!assert prof leaf c130
!assert prof balanced
prof list
prof list sort name 3
prof list sort address asc
prof list sort calls
# the counts survive prof off; reset forgets them
prof off
!assert prof calls c100 3
prof reset
!assert prof routines 0
wprof save profiler.png
