# lag frame counter (lag) on tests/lag/lag.nes (run by ctest: debugger_lag; the program: make_rom.py): its NMI handler
# polls the controller on three frames in four, so any 40 frames hold exactly 10 lag frames
fs 10
lag reset
!assert lag 0
fs 40
!assert lag 10
lag
# a save state brings the count back (the save runs to the next frame: 11, this ROM being what it is)
savestate save 1
!assert lag 11
fs 8
!assert lag min 13
savestate load 1
!assert lag 11
# a soft reset does not zero it, 'lag reset' does
reset
!assert lag min 10
lag reset
!assert lag 0
lag on
lag off
